## RMVPE ONNX pitch estimator

`melody_snake.pitch.rmvpe` adapts the RMVPE feature extraction and decoding
approach from the MIT-licensed RVC/RMVPE implementation by liujing04, 源文雨,
and Ftps, and from NewComer00's MIT-licensed `rmvpe-onnx` package.

The separately downloaded `rmvpe.onnx` model is from
`lj1995/VoiceConversionWebUI` and is identified by its upstream project as MIT
licensed. The model is not included in this repository. Its SHA-256 is
`5370e71ac80af8b4b7c793d27efd51fd8bf962de3a7ede0766dac0befa3660fd`.
